## Berne Convention orientation

For copyrightable original expression, Berne-convention protection is generally based on automatic protection rather than a formality imposed as a condition of protection. Headers, Git object identity, hashes, timestamps and manifests are therefore used as provenance/evidence mechanisms; they do not themselves create copyright.
