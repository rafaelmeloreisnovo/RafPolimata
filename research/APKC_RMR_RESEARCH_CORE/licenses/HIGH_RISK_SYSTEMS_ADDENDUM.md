# High-Risk Systems Addendum

**Coupling ID:** `APKC-RMR-RESEARCH-CORE-V1-20260726`  
**Research grant production authorization:** `NONE`

## Prohibited production use under the research license

The research grant does not authorize production deployment, operational control, diagnosis, treatment, safety interlock or autonomous decision-making in:

- medical devices, imaging, laboratory or clinical systems;
- life support, implants or patient monitoring;
- aviation, space, rail, maritime or autonomous vehicles;
- nuclear, chemical, biological or radiological systems;
- weapons, targeting or lethal-force systems;
- emergency response, public safety or critical communications;
- energy grids, water, telecommunications or other critical infrastructure;
- identity, biometric, credit, employment, education, insurance or legal decisions with significant effects;
- security controls whose failure could cause material harm;
- any system where failure may cause death, personal injury, environmental damage or catastrophic loss.

## Separate authorization gate

High-risk use requires a bespoke signed agreement and, as applicable:

- hazard analysis and safety case;
- independent validation and verification;
- secure development lifecycle;
- SBOM, reproducible build and signed provenance;
- threat modeling and penetration testing;
- human oversight and fail-safe design;
- incident reporting and coordinated vulnerability disclosure;
- rollback and business-continuity drills;
- regulatory approvals and quality-system evidence;
- professional and product-liability insurance;
- acceptance criteria tied to the exact version and deployment environment.

## No reliance

Research artifacts, KATs, hashes, structural APK validation or CI success are not medical, safety, cybersecurity or regulatory certification.
