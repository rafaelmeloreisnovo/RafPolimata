**State:** `TOKEN_VAZIO_FINAL_LEGAL_TEXT`  
**Scope:** original PKC material only; no automatic relicensing of third-party code.  
**claim_allowed:** false  
**Governance:** `CLOSURE_L12`

This file records the owner's requested policy as a **draft governance record**.
It is not represented as a finalized software license or legal opinion.

## 1. Rights/provenance boundary

The Berne Convention is used here as a provenance/copyright reference, not as
the name of a software license.

```text
SOURCE != AUTHORSHIP != COPYRIGHT != LICENSE
PUBLIC_REPOSITORY != PUBLIC_DOMAIN
BERNE_PROTECTION_REFERENCE != SOFTWARE_LICENSE_GRANT
```

Existing upstream notices and licenses remain controlling for imported material.

## 2. Requested grant model to be converted into final legal text

Owner intent recorded in this draft:

- research and scientific study: free use intended;
- education/schools: free use intended;
- social-inclusion uses: free use intended;
- medical, psychological and therapeutic uses: free use intended;
- peace-oriented/public-benefit uses: free use intended;
- commercial exploitation: restricted; requires an official documented grant;
- provenance, documentation and notices: must be preserved;
- use must not erase third-party obligations.

"Scientific/spiritual" is recorded as project ethos and authorship context, not
as a substitute for operative legal clauses.

## 3. Open-source terminology

Because the requested terms distinguish fields/purposes and restrict commercial
use, this draft must **not** be labeled OSI Open Source unless and until its
terms satisfy the Open Source Definition. Public source availability alone does
not establish that status.
